## VISUAL DIRECTION

**Overall:** Keep your established look — alternating dark navy (#0B1220-ish) and off-white slides, oversized coral-to-orange ghost numerals bleeding off the top-right corner, heavy bold sans-serif headlines with one word or phrase in the coral gradient, CIOS badge logo top-left of every slide, thin URL footer bottom-right.

**Slide 01 hero idea:** A backpack and school books on a desk, with a laptop open beside them glowing coral — the visual argument that one of these things is not on the list. Shot desaturated except the screen glow, echoing the black-and-white treatment of your July "Start small, build their future" post.

**Slide 03:** Use real screenshots or clean mockups of actual student projects if you have them — a game screen, a live site, a chart. Authentic student work outperforms stock illustration here.

**Slide 05:** Simple two-column list, coral divider lines between rows. Resist adding icons — the slide is already dense.

**Reel alternative:** If you'd rather post this as a Reel, film a 20-second screen recording of a student project running (game or website) with the caption text appearing as on-screen captions, then cut to the "Comment SEPTEMBER" card.
