THE PATROL DRONE

A guard drone has to march back and forth between two posts — and stop exactly at them, not somewhere past them.

Session 9≈ 20 minutes Velocity & directionIn-class challenge

🚁 The situation

You've seen a ball bounce off the walls of a canvas. But real games rarely bounce things off the screen edge — they bounce them off whatever you decide. A guard walks between two lampposts. A lift stops at two floors. A platform slides between two ledges.

Your drone starts between the two yellow posts and doesn't move, because update() is empty. Fix that.

Your job: make the drone patrol left and right forever between the two posts, turning around exactly at each one — never overlapping a post, never leaving a gap.

Requirements

  1. Move the drone by its velocity every frame.
  2. When it reaches the right post, reverse its direction.
  3. When it reaches the left post, reverse its direction.
  4. It must turn at the post. Watch closely — if it slides past and then jumps back, that's not finished.
⚠️ Two things that will trip you up
The drone is a rectangle, so its x is its left corner. Its right edge is somewhere else — work out where.

And reversing the velocity alone is not enough. Reversing changes where it's going; it doesn't change where it is. If it's already past the post when you flip, it's still past the post on the next frame too.

🚀 If you finish early

  1. Two axes. Give the drone a vy as well, and make it bounce off the top and bottom of the canvas too — so it patrols in a diagonal zig-zag between the posts.
  2. A second drone. Add one starting at the other end with a different speed. Do they need any code to avoid each other? Try it and see what actually happens when they meet.
  3. Speed trap. Make the drone 15% faster every time it turns (drone.vx = drone.vx * 1.15 at each turn — careful with the sign). Let it run. Something breaks. Work out why it breaks, in terms of how far it travels in one frame.
🤔 Think about this one before you leave
Your drone reverses by flipping the sign of a number. What would you have to change to make it stop at the right post and stay there forever instead of bouncing? How much of your code would survive?
