Mini Challenge · Session 11 · Mouse & Touch Input

BLACKOUT

The lights are out in the server room. You have a torch and a mouse. Find three fuses without grabbing a live wire.

Session 11≈ 20 minutes Pointer position & hit testsIn-class challenge

🔦 The situation

The room is pitch black. Your torch follows the mouse and lights a circle around it. Anything inside that circle becomes visible; anything outside it is invisible. Somewhere in the dark are three yellow fuses to click — and two red live wires that end your run.

The drawing code is written. It's already using the mouse position and the torch radius. It just never gets the mouse position, because nothing is listening — so the torch is stuck in the middle of the screen.

Your job: make the torch follow the pointer accurately, and make clicks land on exactly the thing you aimed at.

Requirements

  1. Write getMousePos(event) that converts the event into canvas coordinates.
  2. A mousemove listener that records the position into the mouse object — and does nothing else.
  3. A click listener that finds the item under the pointer using the distance test.
  4. A good item scores; a bad one sets dead to true. An item already found can't be scored twice.
  5. While dead, a click anywhere restarts the game instead — write resetGame() to do it properly.
🔑 Notice what the drawing code is already doing
Look at draw() before you write anything. It calls distance() to decide whether each item is close enough to the torch to be drawn. Your click handler will call the same function to decide whether the pointer is on an item. One function, two completely different jobs, one frame apart.
⚠️ The bug you already know about
If your torch trails behind the pointer by a fixed distance, or your clicks land near-but-not-on things, you know exactly which line is missing. Scroll the page and watch the error grow — that's the proof.

🚀 If you finish early

  1. Shrink the beam. Make the torch a little smaller every second so the room gets harder to search. What has to change for that number to be able to change at all?
  2. Hot and cold. Colour the torch's edge based on how far the pointer is from the nearest unfound fuse — red when far, green when close. You'll need a loop that keeps track of the smallest distance it has seen.
  3. Random every time. On restart, place all five items at random positions instead of the fixed ones. Make sure two items never land on top of each other.
  4. Phone support. Swap your click listener for pointerdown and check it still works with a mouse. What would still be broken on a phone with no hovering pointer?
🤔 Think about this one before you leave
Your click handler and your mousemove handler both work out a position. Could you have used the recorded mouse object in the click handler instead of converting again? Would that always give the same answer — on a touchscreen too?
