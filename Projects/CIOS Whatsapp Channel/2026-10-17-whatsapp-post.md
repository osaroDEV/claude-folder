If you've ever wondered *what actually happens* in a child's first week of learning to code, here's the real list. No jargon.

**5 things your child learns in week one at Code Illustrated Academy:**

1️⃣ **How to make something move on a screen.** A character, a colour, a shape — the first "whoa, I did that" moment. Usually in session one.

2️⃣ **That mistakes aren't failures — they're clues.** A bug is a puzzle, not a verdict. This one quietly reshapes how they handle *every* subject at school.

3️⃣ **How to break a big thing into small steps.** The single most transferable skill coding teaches. Shows up in essays, maths, chores, life.

4️⃣ **That they can build, not just use.** The phone, the game, the website — someone made that. By Friday, your child understands they can be that someone.

5️⃣ **A real, finished thing to show you.** Not a worksheet. Not a certificate. A working *thing* — a game, a page, a tiny app — that they open and run themselves.

That's one week. ⏱️
