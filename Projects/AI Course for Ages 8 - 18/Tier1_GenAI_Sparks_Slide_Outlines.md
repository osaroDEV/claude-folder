## Week 1

### Session A — "AI That Makes vs. AI That Sorts"
1. Title slide: "What Is Generative AI?"
2. "Has AI ever made something brand new for you?" (warm-up question, big text)
3. Example slide: photo app sorting pictures — label "SORTS"
4. Example slide: AI drawing a new picture from words — label "MAKES NEW"
5. "Generative = to make something new" (key vocabulary slide)
6. Activity instructions: "Sort the cards! Sorts or Makes New?"
7. Wrap-up: "Tell us one AI example you know!"

### Session B — "Made by a Person or by AI?"
1. Title slide: "Made By Who?"
2. Recap: two piles from Session A (image)
3. Activity instructions: "Sort the cards onto the mat"
4. Reveal slide: answers with brief "why" notes
5. "Our Clue List" (build live during class, editable slide)
6. Wrap-up: "Next time: How does AI draw?"
