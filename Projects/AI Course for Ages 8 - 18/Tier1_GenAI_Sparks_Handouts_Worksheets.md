## Handout 6.1 — "Real or AI-Made?" Image Set (Week 6, Session A)

Instructor note: prepare 6–8 paired or standalone examples ahead of time (real photographs from a licensed/stock source alongside AI-generated images from an approved tool). Keep all subjects simple and age-appropriate (animals, everyday objects, nature scenes) — no real people's faces.

**Shared clue list (build with class):**
- Hands/fingers that look odd or extra
- Text in the image that looks garbled
- Reflections or shadows that don't match
- Background details that don't quite make sense

## Handout 7.1 — "Safety Rules" Poster (Week 7, Session A)

**Our AI Safety Rules**

1. Never type your name, address, school, or a photo of yourself into any AI tool.
2. Always ask a trusted adult before using a new AI tool.
3. Remember: AI is a tool, not a friend. It doesn't know you and can't take care of you.
4. If an AI's answer seems weird, wrong, or upsetting, tell a trusted adult right away.
5. If you're not sure whether something is safe to share or ask, don't — check with a grown-up first.
