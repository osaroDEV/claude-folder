Generate a fresh, engaging WhatsApp Business Channel post for Code Illustrated Online School targeting PARENTS to register their kids and teenagers.

Context: Code Illustrated teaches beginners (kids and teenagers) how to code websites, games, and other projects. Goal is to convert parents into registering their children for courses.

Requirements:
- Short and snappy (2-4 sentences max for WhatsApp)
- Include relevant emojis
- Vary daily content between:
  * Parent benefits (safe learning, future-proof skills, builds confidence)
  * Success stories from student learners
  * Age-appropriate course offerings
  * Registration incentives or limited offers
  * Problem/solution messaging (kid needs skills → enroll with us)
  * Testimonials from satisfied parents
  * Time investment required (e.g., "30 min/week")
- Keep tone warm, trustworthy, and focused on parent concerns
- Always include a clear call-to-action: "Register now," "Send DM to learn more," "Limited spots available," etc.
- Emphasize: safe learning environment, structured curriculum, proven results, affordable pricing

Format the suggestion clearly so it can be copied directly into WhatsApp. Make each post unique and compelling for parents deciding whether to enroll.
